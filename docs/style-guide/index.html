1

Colours

From the logo's ink and lip splash. One bright colour leads each section; photos sit on white or a light tint; orange means "book"; statuses never rely on colour alone. Contrast is against white unless noted; WCAG AA needs 4.5:1 for normal text.

Logo

Where each version goes: the horizontal lockup in the header of every page; the full logo, large, at the top of the home page only; the full logo in the footer, emails and social posts; the lips icon for the browser tab and small spaces.

Transparent versions made from the master logo. Keep clear space of at least the height of the "M" around it. Never stretch, recolour, crop the wordmark, or place it on a busy photo.

8

Photos

Your work is the star. Natural light, true skin tones, faces in focus; no colour filters. Gallery photos are portrait 4:5; the hero is wide 16:9. Photos sit on white or a light tint, never on bright colour. Every photo gets alt text.
