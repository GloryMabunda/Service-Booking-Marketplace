Style guide

for any service business

The colours, type, shapes and components every page of the booking site uses (#103). Light mode only. Built on Bootstrap 5.3 with tokens.css; the reasoning lives in docs/style-guide.md.

1

Colours

A calm blue with an orange action colour. Change any value in public/css/theme.css to re-colour the whole site. One bright colour leads each section; photos sit on white or a light tint; orange means "book"; statuses never rely on colour alone. Contrast is against white unless noted; WCAG AA needs 4.5:1 for normal text.

7

Logo

The repository ships with no logo. Each business uploads its own in the portal (Website, About tab, Logo). Until it does, the business name is shown as text in the header, like this:

Your Business

Without a logo · the name as text, in the header and the footer

Favicon · the neutral browser tab icon; replace public/images/brand/favicon-64.png with your own

  • Use a PNG with a transparent background, wider than it is tall and at least 1200 px wide. It is shown 48 px tall in the header.
  • Keep clear space around it. Never stretch, recolour or add effects, and don't place it on a strong colour or a busy photo.
  • Describe it for people who can't see it; the default is the business name.

8

Photos

Your work is the star. Natural light, true skin tones, faces in focus; no colour filters. Gallery photos are portrait 4:5; the hero is wide 16:9. Photos sit on white or a light tint, never on bright colour. Every photo gets alt text.
